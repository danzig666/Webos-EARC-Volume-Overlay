## Volume shown in advance

- The watcher learns your soundbar's volume steps from its replies ("at 14, volume up gives 16"). Once a step has been confirmed twice, a key press shows the new level immediately, without the dimmed wait for the soundbar's reply (about 600 ms on a Polk Signa S4).
- When at least four confirmed steps in one direction are all the same size, that size is also used for levels you haven't used yet, so most of the range works after a minute of normal use.
- If the soundbar's reply differs from what was shown, the screen switches to the real number and that step is relearned. A single odd step also turns off the same-size shortcut for that direction.
- Only single presses teach it; when a key is held, it chains predictions from the learned steps and reconciles with the soundbar's answer.
- Learned steps are stored per soundbar, identified by its HDMI-CEC name, in `/var/lib/earc-volume-steps`. A different soundbar starts with its own empty table.
- **Reset learning** on the setup screen clears them, for example after a soundbar firmware update. Uninstalling removes them too.
- Soundbars that never report their level (such as a Samsung HW-Q990B on an LG C2) can't be learned and behave as in 1.5.4.
