## Fix: no learning across a missed soundbar reply

- When the soundbar didn't answer a press, the watcher still assumed the old level, so the next press could teach it a double step (seen on an LG C3 with a Polk Signa S4: `8 down 4` instead of `8 down 6`). After an unanswered press it now learns nothing until the soundbar reports its level again.
- A wrong step learned this way is corrected automatically the next time that step is used, which also restores the same-size shortcut for that direction. **Reset learning** clears it at once.
